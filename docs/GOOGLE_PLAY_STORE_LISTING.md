## Full Description (4000 characters max)
Farm2Market Uganda is a comprehensive agricultural trading platform designed to connect farmers and traders across Uganda. Our mission is to empower the agricultural community by facilitating fair, transparent, and efficient trade.

**Key Features:**

🌾 **For Farmers:**
- List your produce with ease
- Set your prices and negotiate directly with buyers
- Track your inventory and sales
- Receive real-time notifications about offers and transactions
- Secure payment processing through Pesapal integration

📊 **For Traders:**
- Browse available produce from verified farmers
- Make competitive offers and negotiate prices
- Manage your inventory and storage locations
- Track deliveries and transactions
- Access detailed reports and analytics

🔔 **Push Notifications:**
- Stay updated with real-time notifications
- Receive alerts about new offers, transactions, and important updates
- Never miss an opportunity

📱 **User-Friendly Interface:**
- Mobile-optimized design for easy navigation
- Collapsible sections to reduce scrolling
- Prominent display of delivery locations
- Responsive layout for all screen sizes

🔒 **Secure & Reliable:**
- Secure authentication and data protection
- Reliable cloud infrastructure
- Regular backups and data safety

**Why Farm2Market Uganda?**

We understand the challenges faced by farmers and traders in Uganda's agricultural sector. Our platform bridges the gap between producers and buyers, creating opportunities for growth and prosperity.

Whether you're a small-scale farmer looking to reach more buyers or a trader seeking quality produce, Farm2Market Uganda provides the tools and platform you need to succeed.

**Join thousands of farmers and traders already using Farm2Market Uganda to grow their business!**

Download now and start trading today.
